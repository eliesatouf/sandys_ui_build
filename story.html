OUR JOURNEY

Create One Experience

The story of how a Filipino chef's passion for Indian cuisine led to Metro City's most unique dining experience.

Our Humble Beginnings

In 2022, after years of perfecting recipes and dreaming of sharing our unique fusion concept, Sandys Restaurant opened its doors. What began as a simple idea—blending Filipino warmth with Indian spice traditions—has become Metro City's most celebrated culinary fusion experience.

🇵🇭

Filipino Roots

Sandys Restaurant finds its soul in the vibrant food culture of the Philippines. Our foundation is built upon the Filipino tradition of kapwa—shared identity and community. Just as Filipino meals bring people together around a communal table, we've designed Sandys to be a place where food creates connections.

The warmth of Filipino hospitality, the communal nature of dining, and the bold flavors of dishes like adobo and sisig became the foundation of what would later become Sandys.

🍲

"Food is the memory we share with those we love."

🇮🇳

Indian Inspiration

A life-changing trip to India introduced Frederick to a world of spices he never knew existed. For six months, he apprenticed in Mumbai kitchens, learning the ancient art of spice blending and the slow, deliberate cooking methods that define Indian cuisine.

It was there that he discovered the magic of biryani—not just as a dish, but as a culinary philosophy. Layers of flavor, patience in preparation, and the celebration of complexity.

🫕

"Spices are the alphabet of flavor."

✨

The Fusion Moment

Back in Manila, Frederick began experimenting. What if sisig could borrow the complexity of Indian spices? What if butter chicken could embrace Filipino cooking techniques? After two years of testing, tasting, and refining, the concept for Sandys was born.
