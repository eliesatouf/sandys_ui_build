Frequently Asked Questions

Do you accept event bookings?

Yes! Sandys Cafe is perfect for birthdays, meetings, and special occasions. Contact us to discuss your event requirements and availability.

Is there parking available?

Yes, we have dedicated parking space for our customers. Additional street parking is also available nearby.

Do you have vegetarian/vegan options?

Yes! We offer several vegetarian options and can accommodate dietary restrictions. Please inform our staff about any allergies or dietary needs.

Can I work remotely from the cafe?

Absolutely! We have free high-speed WiFi, power outlets at every table, and a comfortable working environment. Feel free to stay as long as you like.

Do you offer takeout or delivery?

Yes, we offer both takeout and delivery through our partners. You can also call ahead to place your order for pickup.
