Our Story

Sandys Cafe began with a simple vision: to create a welcoming space in Kalibo where people could enjoy quality coffee, sweet treats, and create beautiful memories together.

The Beginning

Opened in 2023, Sandys Cafe quickly became a favorite spot in Kalibo for coffee lovers and those seeking a cozy place to work, meet, or relax.

Community Focus

We believe a cafe should be more than just a place to drink coffee—it should be a hub for community gatherings, events, and connections.

Quality Promise

Every cup of coffee and every pastry is prepared with care, using quality ingredients to ensure our customers have the best experience possible.

Our Founders & Story

Born from a shared passion for coffee and community, Sandys Cafe was founded in 2023 to bring something special to Kalibo.

Dominic

Founder

"Kalibo deserves great coffee"

With a background in hospitality management and a passion for specialty coffee, Alex envisioned creating a space where quality meets comfort. Having traveled extensively to coffee regions, Alex brings global coffee culture to Kalibo.

Stephany

Co-Founder

"Every sweet tells a story"

A local baker with a creative flair, Maya believes desserts should be both beautiful and delicious. She develops all the pastry recipes in-house, using local ingredients whenever possible to support Aklan's producers.

Our Journey

2022

The Idea Was Born

Alex and Maya shared their dream over coffee: to create Kalibo's go-to cafe that combines excellent coffee with homemade sweets and a welcoming atmosphere.

2023

Sandys Cafe Opens

After months of preparation, Sandys Cafe opened its doors on Roxas Avenue. The response from the Kalibo community was immediate and overwhelmingly positive.

2024

Expanding Our Reach

Launching our website to share our cafe experience online. Continuing to host community events and introduce new menu items based on customer feedback.

Future

What's Next

Planning to introduce coffee workshops, expand our event hosting capabilities, and continue being a beloved part of Kalibo's food and community scene.

Why "Sandys"?

Our name carries our philosophy

Welcome

"Sandys" means "welcome" or "come in" in the local language. Our doors are always open to everyone.

Stay Awhile

We encourage you to relax, work, meet friends, or simply enjoy a moment of peace with good coffee.

Feel at Home

We want every visitor to feel like they're in their own living room—comfortable, cared for, and valued.
